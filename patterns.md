# Patterns — Scaling Up (Harnish)

## 4 Decisions Assessment (pick where to start)
**When to use**: before choosing any tool; the team is overwhelmed.
**How**: leaders score People, Strategy, Execution, Cash; start with the weakest; implement one chapter per month for a quarter.
**Trade-offs**: leaders drift to the decision they like; expect 2-3 years to become DNA, 2-3 more to master.

## FACe / PACe (accountability charts)
**When to use**: unclear ownership, founder bottleneck, functions nobody owns.
**How**: list functions with no titles; ONE Person Accountable, 1-3 KPIs each; flag empty boxes and any name in 3-4+ boxes. PACe: 4-9 key processes, one owner, KPIs for time, cost, quality.
**Trade-offs**: shared accountability looks safe but creates gaps.

## Job Scorecard + Topgrading hiring
**When to use**: any key hire.
**How**: scorecard (purpose, 3-10 outcomes for 1-3 years, competencies) → recruit like guerilla marketing → 20:1 applicants → CIDS interview with TORC → rank Will, Values, Results, Skill.
**Trade-offs**: slower than gut hiring; gut hits only 25-60%.

## Five management practices (coach in one-on-ones)
**When to use**: past ~50-100 employees, when charisma stops scaling.
**How**: hire fewer/pay more; recognition at 3:1; line of sight; dehassle; strengths (love/loathe); 20-30 min weekly one-on-one.
**Trade-offs**: needs trained middle managers first (train them before frontline).

## Core alive (8 Ways)
**When to use**: culture drift near 50-70 people or after an acquisition.
**How**: align storytelling, hiring, onboarding, appraisals, recognition, etc. around one list of 3-8 Values; run a Core Values Hall of Fame.
**Trade-offs**: wall posters without HR alignment do nothing.

## 7 Strata of Strategy
**When to use**: strategy is vague or margins erode.
**How**: Words You Own → Sandbox + 3 Brand Promises → Promise Guarantee → One-PHRASE → 3-5 Differentiating Activities → X-Factor → Profit per X + BHAG; run through a weekly Council.
**Trade-offs**: never reveal the One-PHRASE or X-Factor outside.

## OPSP planning cycle
**When to use**: quarterly and annual planning.
**How**: collect Start/Stop/Keep input; middle managers do SWOT + top 3; senior team does SWT + top 3; fill columns 1-7; pick Critical Number, 3-5 Rocks, Theme.
**Trade-offs**: ship a good plan now; iterate quarterly.

## Quarterly Theme + celebration
**When to use**: every 90 days (start with quarterly, not annual).
**How**: Super Green/Green/Red tiers, an employee Champion builds the theme, reward fun first. On a miss: repeat, move on, or root-cause one fix. Ask "How did you do it?".
**Trade-offs**: do not move the goal mid-quarter or tie pay to unproven targets.

## Weekly feedback loop (Habits #5, #6, #9, #10)
**When to use**: leaders lack market and employee signal.
**How**: each manager weekly runs one Start/Stop/Keep and one 4Q talk; salespeople leave a 3-min voicemail; every person has 1-2 KPIs; scoreboards visible.
**Trade-offs**: collecting feedback without closing the loop kills it.

## Meeting Rhythm
**When to use**: misalignment, slow decisions.
**How**: daily huddle ≤15 min (specifics, no problem-solving); weekly 30-90 min with WWW and one-phrase close; monthly management day (DNA transfer); quarterly/annual offsites.
**Trade-offs**: generalities kill huddles.

## CASh brainstorm and daily cash report
**When to use**: growth is draining cash.
**How**: split the CCC into selling, billing, production, inventory cycles; ideas = shorten cycles, remove mistakes, change the model; CFO reports 24h cash daily.
**Trade-offs**: relaxing after a fix brings it back.

## Simple Numbers and grow cycle
**When to use**: profit target and hiring pace.
**How**: clear distortions (market owner pay; Gross Margin Dollars); targets 10% good, 15% great; LER sets hiring; hold labor flat to 15%, add, never below 10%; waterfall by customer/product.
**Trade-offs**: commission on revenue pays for volume, not margin.

## Power of One + Return on Net Assets
**When to use**: pick financial levers, talk to the bank.
**How**: size 1% / 1-day moves on the 7 levers; DuPont: EBIT/revenue × revenue/net assets; assign a lever owner with a KPI.
**Trade-offs**: working-capital levers hold most cash; do not move all 7 at once.
