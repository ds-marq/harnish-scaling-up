## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Structure: the 4 Decisions.** Every growth problem is a People, Strategy, Execution or Cash problem. Use the 4 Decisions Assessment to find the weakest one and start there; work one chapter per month. Never implement everything at once. Expect 2-3 years to make the tools company DNA; name a driver (often the #2).

**People**
- Use the **FACe** (Function Accountability Chart) when ownership is unclear: titles omitted, ONE Person Accountable per function, 1-3 KPIs. Empty boxes and one name in 3-4+ boxes are the failure signals. **PACe** does the same for 4-9 key processes.
- Replace job descriptions with a **Job Scorecard** (purpose, 3-10 outcomes for 1-3 years, competencies). Hire with **Topgrading**: ≥20 applicants per opening, CIDS interview with TORC, criteria ranked **Will, Values, Results, Skill (WVRS)**.
- Coach with the **five management practices**: hire fewer/pay more, recognition 3:1, line of sight, dehassling, strengths (love/loathe). Replace annual reviews with weekly one-on-ones. Train middle managers first.
- Define **Core Values** (3-8, discovered not invented), **Core Purpose** and **Core Competencies**; bring them alive through all HR systems (**8 Ways**). Culture drifts at ~50-70 people.

**Strategy**
- **7 Strata of Strategy**: Words You Own → Sandbox + Brand Promises → Brand Promise Guarantee (Catalytic Mechanism) → One-PHRASE Strategy → Differentiating Activities → X-Factor → Profit per X + BHAG. Run it through a weekly Strategic Thinking Team ("the Council").
- Use **SWT** (Strengths, Weaknesses, Trends) with the senior team, SWOT with middle managers.
- The **One-Page Strategic Plan (OPSP)** has 7 columns: Values (Should/Shouldn't), Purpose/Profit per X/BHAG (Why), 3-5 year targets and Key Thrusts (Where), 12-month Critical Number and Key Initiatives (What), 90-day Critical Number and Rocks (How), Theme and Finish Lines (Fun), individual KPIs (Who). Small firms use the **Vision Summary**.

**Execution**
- The 10 **Rockefeller Habits**: #1 healthy executive team; #2 everyone aligned on the #1 priority (Critical Number); #3 meeting rhythm; #4 accountability for every facet; #5 employee input; #6 customer input; #7 Core Values and Purpose alive; #8 strategy articulated by everyone; #9 KPIs for everyone; #10 scoreboards. Install one or two per quarter.
- Set the **Critical Number** and 3-5 **Rocks** each quarter with Super Green/Green/Red tiers and a **Quarterly Theme** built by an employee Champion. Never move the goal mid-quarter.
- Meeting rhythm: daily huddle ≤15 min (specifics, no problem-solving), weekly meeting with **WWW** (Who, What, When), monthly management day (DNA transfer), quarterly/annual offsites.
- Weekly feedback: Start/Stop/Keep with employees, 4Q conversation with customers, NPS, salesperson voicemail call-in. Close the loop on every suggestion.

**Cash**
- **Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)** and **CASh** brainstorm: shorten cycles, eliminate mistakes, change the business model; CFO daily cash report. "Growth sucks cash."
- **Simple Numbers**: clear distortions (Gross Margin Dollars as top line), profit targets (10% good, 15% great), **Labor Efficiency Ratio (LER)** drives hiring, 4 Forces of Cash Flow (Core Capital Target = 2 months of opex).
- **Power of One**: 1% or 1-day move on 7 levers (price, volume, COGS, operating expenses, AR, inventory/WIP, AP); **Return on Net Assets** via DuPont; Marginal Cash Flow.

**Barriers (ch02)**: leadership (Prediction, Delegation, Repetition), scalable infrastructure, marketing. Complexity grows faster than headcount; Valleys of Death sit between headcount clusters. Prefer the 20-Mile March.
