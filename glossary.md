# Glossary — Scaling Up (Harnish)

**4 Decisions** — People, Strategy, Execution, Cash; the book's organizing structure (Ch 0-1)
**4D Framework** — Driver, Demands, Disciplines, Decisions (Ch 1)
**4Q conversation** — weekly in-person customer talk (Rockefeller Habit #6) (Ch 10)
**5Fs** — Faith, Family, Friends, Fitness, Finance, inside the OPPP (Ch 3)
**20-Mile March** — steady, predictable growth beats wild swings (Ch 2)
**A Player** — top 10% of the available talent that will accept your offer (Ch 4)
**BHAG** — 10-25 year measurable goal tied to Purpose and Profit per X (Ch 1, 7)
**Brand Promises** — three measurable reasons to buy, one leading; tracked by Kept Promise Indicators (Ch 7)
**Brand Promise Guarantee (Catalytic Mechanism)** — a guarantee that makes breaking a promise hurt (Ch 7)
**Cash Acceleration Strategies (CASh)** — one-page brainstorm to shorten the CCC (Ch 12)
**Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)** — days between spending a dollar and getting it back as cash (Ch 12)
**CIDS interview** — Chronological In-Depth Structured interview (Ch 4)
**Core Capital Target** — two months of operating expenses in cash after taxes, line of credit undrawn (Ch 13)
**Core Competencies** — hard-to-copy, reusable strengths customers feel (Ch 6)
**Core Purpose** — the "Why", centered on one word or idea (Ch 6)
**Core Values** — 3-8 rules that define personality; the Should/Shouldn't test (Ch 6, 8)
**Critical Number** — the one measure that matters most this quarter or year (Ch 8, 9)
**Daily Huddle** — 5-15 min stand-up: what's up, metrics, stucks (Ch 11)
**Dehassling** — removing demotivators, mainly people and processes (Ch 5)
**Empty box** — a function nobody owns on the FACe (Ch 3)
**FACe** — Function Accountability Chart (Ch 3)
**Gazelle** — high-growth firm scaling beyond $10M, $100M or $1B (Ch 0, 1)
**Gross Margin Dollars** — the true internal top line, instead of revenue (Ch 13)
**Growth sucks cash** — first law of business dynamics (Ch 1, 8)
**Habit #1-#10** — the 10 Rockefeller Habits (Ch 1, 9, 10, 11)
**Job Scorecard** — outcomes and competencies, replacing the job description (Ch 4)
**Key Thrusts** — 3-5 major priorities over 3-5 years, OPSP column 3 (Ch 8)
**Labor Efficiency Ratio (LER)** — productivity multiplier of each labor dollar (Ch 13)
**Line of sight** — employee can link daily work to Purpose, strategy, Promise (Ch 5, 10)
**Marginal Cash Flow** — gross margin cents vs. working-capital cents per sales dollar (Ch 14)
**Meeting Rhythm** — daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual meetings (Ch 11)
**NPS** — Net Promoter Score; measures advocacy (Ch 10)
**OPPP** — One-Page Personal Plan: Relationships, Achievements, Rituals, Wealth (Ch 3)
**One-PHRASE Strategy** — the business-model lever that drives profit (Ch 7)
**OPSP** — One-Page Strategic Plan, 7 columns (Ch 8)
**PACe** — Process Accountability Chart, 4-9 key processes (Ch 3)
**Power of One** — impact of 1% (or 1 day) on each of the 7 levers (Ch 14)
**Profit per X** — single economic-engine KPI (Ch 7)
**Quarterly Theme** — fun framing of the quarter's Critical Number (Ch 8, 9)
**Return on Net Assets** — EBIT / net operating assets; DuPont split (Ch 14)
**Rockefeller Habits** — 10 execution routines (Ch 1, 9)
**Rocks** — 3-5 quarterly priorities, each with one owner (Ch 8, 9)
**Sandbox** — what you sell, to whom, where (Ch 7)
**7 Strata of Strategy** — framework for dominating an industry (Ch 7)
**7 Levers** — price, volume, COGS, operating expenses, AR, inventory/WIP, AP (Ch 14)
**Simple Numbers** — 4 keys to profitability and cash (Ch 13)
**Start/Stop/Keep** — employee/customer feedback question set (Ch 8, 10)
**Strategic Thinking Team ("Council")** — advisory team that talks weekly with customers and competitors (Ch 7)
**Super Green / Green / Red** — stretch, target, minimum goal tiers (Ch 9)
**SWT** — Strengths, Weaknesses, Trends; senior-team replacement for SWOT (Ch 8)
**Topgrading** — hiring method; right hire 90%+ vs. 25-60% by gut (Ch 4)
**Valleys of Death** — headcount gaps where you outgrow the current talent and systems (Ch 2)
**Vision Summary** — simplified one-pager for small firms (Ch 8)
**WAG** — wild-ankle guess between BHAG and the next 90 days (Ch 1, 9)
**WVRS** — Will, Values, Results, Skill, in that order, for hiring (Ch 4)
**WWW** — Who, What, When; the only meeting notes needed (Ch 11)
**X-Factor** — 10x-100x hidden advantage on an industry choke point (Ch 7)
