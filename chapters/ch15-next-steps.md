# Chapter 15: Next Steps — 5 Things to Do Now

## Core Idea
Start implementing Scaling Up with five concrete actions, one step at a time. Success belongs to those with an insatiable desire to learn and an unquenchable bias for action.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **5 Things to Do Now**: the book's jump-start sequence.
  - When to use: right after reading, to avoid doing everything at once.
  - How: (1) Get the whole leadership team (and employees) to read or listen to the book, take the 4 Decisions Assessment, pick the first of People, Strategy, Execution, or Cash, and implement one chapter per month for a quarter. (2) Form a weekly council: a few key leaders discuss strategy and bigger opportunities; work the 7 Strata of Strategy worksheet, list influencers, and the 4Ps or 4Es of marketing. (3) Launch a Quarterly Theme: a measurable goal on a choke point, tracked on a whiteboard via the daily huddle, with a celebration at the end; make it challenging but doable. (4) Start the daily huddle with the executive team, then cascade; 15 minutes or less, share specifics, do not problem-solve. (5) Plan the first quarterly or annual offsite (ideally facilitated): employee and customer surveys, SWOT by middle management, SWT by top team; then fill the one-page Growth Tools starting with the Vision Summary (Core Values, Purpose, Brand Promises, BHAG, Priorities) and pick a couple of Rockefeller Habits.
- **4D Framework**: work on one of the four decisions (People, Strategy, Execution, Cash) at a time so no one is overwhelmed.
  - How: expect two to three years for the tools to become company DNA and two to three more to master them; appoint one driver (often the #2 in command).

## Key Concepts
- **Rockefeller Habits**: the checklist of habits whose adoption defines the system.
- **Quarterly Theme**: a company-wide measurable goal for the quarter.
- **Daily huddle**: short daily stand-up for rhythm and communication.
- **Weekly council**: leaders' strategic talk time, separate from the weekly management meeting.
- **4 Decisions Assessment**: diagnoses which of the four areas to start with.
- **Support stack**: coaching, workshops and certification, and Align and Better Book Club software.

## Mental Models
- Use "one chapter per month" when the team is overwhelmed by the full toolkit.
- Use a visible, winnable Theme when you need early momentum: quick success builds buy-in.
- Use a named implementation owner (the #2) when accountability for the system is unclear.

## Anti-patterns
- **Doing everything at once**: overwhelms the team and stalls adoption.
- **Problem-solving in the huddle**: turns a 15-minute sync into a meeting.
- **Fancy Themes that delay launch**: a whiteboard and a daily huddle are enough.
- **Founder carrying implementation alone**: assign a driver and consider a coach.

## Key Takeaways
1. Share a common language: everyone reads the book and takes the assessment.
2. Start with the weakest of the four decisions.
3. Weekly council, Quarterly Theme, daily huddle, and offsite are the first rhythms.
4. Implement slowly: years to embed, years to master.
5. Learning plus action is the trait that predicts success.
